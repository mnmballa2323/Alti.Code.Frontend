# Inso.Code Vision

## 🌌 The "Universe" Philosophy
Inso.Code is built on the belief that **AI should not just assist developers; it should participate as a peer.**

We are moving away from "Copilots" (passive assistance) to "Agents" (active participants).

### The "Triumvirate" of Power
1.  **Creation (The Architect)**: The Developer's domain. Unfettered access to filesystem, terminal, and compilers. Local, fast, private.
2.  **Governance (The Sentinel)**: The Admin's domain. Policy enforcement, security scanning, and role management.
3.  **Strategy (The Overseer)**: The Business Owner's domain. Cost analysis, velocity metrics, and resource allocation.

## 🎯 Ultimate Goal
To build a **Self-Evolving Development Environment (SEDE)** where:
-   The IDE notices a bug, writes a test case, fixes the bug, and pushes the commit—all while the human developer sleeps.
-   The system automatically optimizes its own cloud infrastructure costs based on usage patterns.
-   Security policies are not just documents but active code that prevents vulnerabilities from ever being committed.

## 📐 Design Principles
1.  **Local-First, Cloud-Aware**: Developers work locally for speed; Managers oversee globally for control.
2.  **Compliance by Default**: Security is not an afterthought; it is baked into the scaffolding.
3.  **Agent Specialization**: Instead of one "God AI", we have specialized agents (The Weaver, The Polyglot, etc.) that excel at specific tasks.
